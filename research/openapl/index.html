Research / Identity and access

OpenAPL

Access policy language for humans and AI agents.

Version 0.1.0Released 2026-11-19

The problem

A support agent hands a refund to a refund agent; the gateway sees only the last hop, so nobody can say which human it's for or what a hijacked agent could reach.

What's new

Evaluates the whole delegation chain, from the human through every agent, and returns ALLOW, DENY or REQUIRE_APPROVAL with the entitlements and control references behind it. No LLM sits in the decision path.

My role

Sole author: designed, built and maintain it.
